On the bench

Not in a single list

Source. HSGuru deck and archetype stats for Standard with post-ban filters, minimum 100 games per list, read from screenshots on 24 Oct 2026: . Card data and art from HearthstoneJSON.

Shares. Every list is weighted by games × (win rate − 50), so a 6,000-game list at 63% counts far more than a 100-game list at 58%. A card is core in a deck at 80% share or more, and flex from 30% to 80%.

Verdicts. Craft = core in a deck with at least three lists, a 56%+ win rate, and at least 5% of the meta weight. One deck = core somewhere smaller. Optional = flex at best. Wait = barely played. Skip = played, but the deck that uses it is beaten by a same-class version without it.

"Also needs" lists the other epics and legendaries that are core in that deck and cost dust. Free cards are left out: the Core set, event cards, reward-track cards and cards granted for opening packs or finishing the starter quests, all marked FREE. Cards that are free only in golden still count as crafts, and Fabled bonus cards come with their legendary. Dust assumes one copy of a legendary (1,600) and two of an epic (2 × 400).

Limits. The set is four days old and these are HSGuru's top-performing lists, not every deck played. Card names were read by eye from screenshots. One Top 5k Void C'Thun DH list was left out because its win rate was cut off.
