Shares. Each archetype is one deck. Every list is weighted by games × (win rate − 50), so a 6,000-game list at 63% counts far more than a 100-game list at 58%. A card's share is the weighted fraction of the deck's lists that run it.

Tiers. Core = 80%+ share in a deck with two or more lists, at least 30% in another deck, and at least 2% of all weighted play. Solid = core in one deck with two or more lists. Fringe = 30%+ somewhere, or core only in a one-list deck. Trash = the rest. N'Zoth, Dream's End is held at Fringe: it's played, but the Handbuff DK that skips it wins more.

Free cards cost no dust and never count as something a deck "needs": the Core set, event cards, reward-track cards and cards granted for opening packs or finishing the starter quests. Cards that are free only in golden still count as crafts. Dust assumes one copy of a legendary (1,600) and two of an epic (2 × 400).

Limits. The expansion is days old and these are HSGuru's top-performing lists, not every deck played. Card names were read by eye from screenshots. One Top 5k Void C'Thun DH list was left out because its win rate was cut off.
