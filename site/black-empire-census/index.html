Every deck loses ground at the higher level. Aggro Warlock stays on top; C'Thun DH, the most-played deck, drops furthest.

The N'Zoth trap

N'Zoth, Dream's End is the card that turns Handbuff DK into N'Zoth Handbuff DK. The version without it, and with no Black Empire epics or legendaries at all, wins more at both levels.

Method. Each list is weighted by games × (win rate − 50). A card is core where it carries at least 80% of an archetype's weight, flex at 30–80%, and fringe below that or in only one or two lists.

Limits. The set is four days old and these are HSGuru's top-performing lists, not every deck played. Card names were read by eye from screenshots. One Top 5k Void C'Thun DH list was left out because its win rate was cut off.
